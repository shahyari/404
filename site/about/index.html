About

{{siteName}} is an open-source collection of creative and experimental takes on the most common dead end on the web: the “page not found” screen.

Some are a single line of type. Some are animations, toys, or tiny games. They have one thing in common: you arrived somewhere that doesn’t exist, and it turned out to be worth a moment.

How it works

Every experience is a standalone web page with its own HTML, CSS and JavaScript. It runs inside an isolated, sandboxed frame. It can look and behave however it likes, and it cannot affect this website or any other experience.

There is no backend, no tracking and no analytics. Everything runs in your browser.

Use them

Every experience can be reused for free on your own site. Unless an experience says otherwise, it is released under the MIT License. Its author and license are listed with it.

Contribute

Have an idea for a 404 page? Copy the template, build your experience in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and open a pull request. You don’t need to know how the rest of the site works.
